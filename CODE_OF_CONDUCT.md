# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in AlphaFun a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or other personal
characteristic.

We expect participants to act professionally, listen in good faith, give and
receive constructive feedback, and prioritize the wellbeing of the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information without
permission, deliberate disruption, and any conduct that would be inappropriate
in a professional project space.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct concerns in a public issue. Contact the project
maintainers privately through the repository's private contact mechanism or a
private message to a maintainer listed in [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS).
Reports will be reviewed respectfully and kept as confidential as practical.
Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, restrict participation,
or take other action they consider appropriate.

This document is adapted from the Contributor Covenant and is intended to be
applied in the spirit of a safe, inclusive, and constructive community.
